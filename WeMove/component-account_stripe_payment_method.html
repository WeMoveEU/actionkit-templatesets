{% comment %} Payment method label for a recurring donation created by donations-api (account "WM-donations"). The profile itself carries no payment method (card_num/exp_date are dummies, order.payment_method is always "cc"); the real one is the custom action field `stripe_payment_method_type` on the profile's donation action, written on signup and on every method swap.
