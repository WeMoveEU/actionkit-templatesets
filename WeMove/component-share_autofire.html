{% comment %} Fires the share the supporter already picked on the page they came from. A share step cannot read another page's followup text or custom fields, so rather than copy that content it links here as ?share= -- the page being shared renders its own button, and this clicks it. The bundle's own handlers (send-share-to-action-kit.js, taf-button.js) then do the tracking and the navigation, so nothing here duplicates what they already do. wm-scriptsLoaded fires at the end of initAkScripts(), after initShare() has bound those handlers -- see wemove-main.js in pubstatic. This script is inline in the body, so the listener is registered long before the event. {% endcomment %}
